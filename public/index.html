Jonaki Pothe is a free, community-run safety platform for Siliguri. Report hazards on your street, discover safe spots, and get help fast. Every small light — every report — makes the city safer for everyone.

Reports are community-submitted and unverified. Always call 112 in an emergency.

How it works

Three simple steps to make Siliguri's streets safer.

📍

Report a hazard

Spot a broken streetlight, waterlogging, or stray dogs? Drop a pin and describe it. Takes 30 seconds.

✅

Confirm reports

See a report near you? Tap "Confirm" to verify it's real. More confirmations = more trust.

🆘

SOS in one tap

Logged-in users get a large red SOS button. It calls 112 and messages your saved contacts with your GPS location.

Built for Siliguri, by Siliguri

Jonaki Pothe is a free, community-driven project. It is not affiliated with any government agency. Data is stored securely on Firebase (Google) and never sold. Reports are reviewed by volunteer admins before they appear publicly.
