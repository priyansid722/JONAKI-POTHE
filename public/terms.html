About this service

Jonaki Pothe ("the platform") is a free, volunteer-run community safety reporting service for Siliguri, West Bengal. By using this platform, you agree to these Terms of Service.

How you may use this service

  • View the safety map and approved reports without signing in.
  • Create an account using Google Sign-In to post reports, confirm sightings, and use SOS features.
  • Post honest, accurate reports about places and conditions you have personally encountered.
  • Use the SOS feature to quickly contact emergency services and your saved contacts.

Content rules (what you must NOT post)

  • No personal information of others — do not name, describe, photograph, or single out any individual or group of people.
  • No hate speech — do not post content that discriminates or incites hatred based on religion, caste, gender, ethnicity, or any other characteristic.
  • No false or misleading reports — only report hazards you have genuinely observed.
  • No spam or duplicates — do not post the same or similar report many times.
  • No commercial use — do not use the platform to promote businesses or services.
  • No illegal content — do not post anything that violates Indian law.

Community rule: Describe places and conditions — never individuals or communities.
