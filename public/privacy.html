Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Jonaki Pothe is a free, volunteer-run community safety platform open to everyone. We are not affiliated with any government agency. Our backend is powered by Firebase (Google).

What we collect

  • Name and email address — when you sign in with Google. We use these to identify your account and display your name on non-anonymous reports.
  • Safety reports — the category, description, and location coordinates you enter when you post a report.
  • Location — only when you press the SOS button or tap "Use my location" on the map. We never track your location continuously or in the background.
  • Emergency contacts — names and phone numbers you save yourself in the Emergency Contacts settings. Stored privately under your account.
  • SOS events — when you trigger SOS, we save your uid, coordinates, and timestamp. This is visible only to you and admins.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your password (handled entirely by Google Sign-In — we never see it).
  • Your phone contacts beyond what you explicitly save in Emergency Contacts.
  • Your browsing history, device identifiers, or any tracking cookies.
  • Payment information of any kind.

How we use your information

  • To display your reports on the community map (after admin review).
  • To power the SOS feature — prefilling emergency messages with your location.
  • To enforce rate limits (maximum 10 reports per user per day).
  • To allow admins to review and moderate content.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Your data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud), which is our hosting and database provider. Firebase's own privacy policy applies to data stored on their servers: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Approved safety reports (description, category, location, time of day) are visible to the public. Your name and email are never part of the public report — only the content you chose to write. If you use the anonymous option, even your display name is hidden.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion (see below), we will delete your profile, emergency contacts, SOS events, and author records within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Access: You can view your own reports at My Reports.
  • Deletion: Email us at privacy@jonakipothe.in to request complete deletion of your data. We will process requests within 30 days.
  • Correction: Contact us if you believe we hold inaccurate data about you.

Cookies and local storage

Firebase Authentication stores a session token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. This is essential for the sign-in feature and is not used for tracking or advertising. We do not use any third-party tracking cookies.

Children

Jonaki Pothe is not intended for users under the age of 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us at privacy@jonakipothe.in.

About community reports

⚠️ Disclaimer: Reports on Jonaki Pothe are submitted by community members and are unverified. They are reviewed by volunteer admins before appearing publicly, but we cannot guarantee their accuracy, completeness, or timeliness. Do not rely solely on this platform for safety decisions. Always call 112 in a real emergency.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, email: privacy@jonakipothe.in
